## What is xScaler?

xScaler is a drop-in backend for any OpenTelemetry-compatible stack. You push
telemetry from your existing Prometheus, Grafana Alloy, OpenTelemetry Collector,
or OTel SDKs. xScaler handles ingestion, storage, and querying at scale across
all three signals. You query it back with **PromQL** (metrics), **LogQL** (logs),
and **TraceQL** (traces) from Grafana or any compatible tool.
